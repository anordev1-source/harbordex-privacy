Harbordex Android background notifications

R6TLabs · com.r6tlabs.harbordex · version 0.1.0 (16)

Recorded from the signed Android release build. The demonstration shows notification permission, enabling Background notifications, starting a task, receiving its completion alert while Harbordex is in the background, and the disabled setting.

The paired computer uses a fictional project and a scripted agent fixture. The connection, Android foreground service and completion notification are real. Idle intervals were removed; no app UI was fabricated. The notification's Stop action is not demonstrated.
